chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano Set to Enabled
chrome://flags/#optimization-guide-on-device-model Set to Enabled BypassPerfRequirement

Click Relaunch at the bottom of the flags page. To ensure Chrome finishes downloading the Nano model, check chrome://components and look for Optimization Guide On Device Model.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions? Find answers on how YouTube Study Filter works, its privacy protections, and AI engines.

How does YouTube Study Filter decide what to blur?

The extension evaluates the public video title text against educational criteria. Content such as coding tutorials, math lectures, science demonstrations, and courses are kept visible. Entertainment, gaming, vlogs, reactions, and drama are blurred.

Is YouTube Study Filter completely free?

Yes, 100% free! You can use on-device Gemini Nano with zero costs or use Google AI Studio's free tier (15 requests per minute), which is more than enough for regular YouTube studying.

Does the extension track my YouTube watch history?

Never. The extension runs in title-only mode. It never touches your user profile, search queries, watch history, subscriptions, or comments. Your browsing activity stays completely private.

Can I temporarily unblur a video if I want to watch it?

Yes! Every blurred card has a glassmorphic "Show" button. Clicking it unblurs that specific video for your current session without modifying its persistent filter classification.

Which browsers are compatible?

Any modern Chromium browser that supports Manifest V3, including Google Chrome, Brave, and Microsoft Edge.

How is this different from generic YouTube blockers?

Generic blockers hide everything or block the whole site. YouTube Study Filter is intelligent: it keeps educational lectures and skills tutorials visible while filtering out time-wasting distractions in real time.

COMPLIANCE & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

YouTube Study Filter ("we", "the Extension") is dedicated to respecting and preserving your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when using our extension.

1. Single Purpose & Data Processing

The single purpose of the Extension is to evaluate public YouTube video titles to filter and blur non-educational content during study sessions. The extension operates in title-only mode; video thumbnails, video streams, audio, comments, search history, user accounts, and cookies are never accessed or stored.

2. AI Execution Modes

  • On-Device Gemini Nano: Video title strings are sent directly to Chrome's local `window.ai` Prompt API session. All computation runs 100% locally on your computer. Zero data leaves your machine.
  • Direct Gemini API: If you choose to enter your Google AI Studio API key, video titles are sent directly to Google's official Generative Language endpoints (https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using TLS encryption. No intermediate servers, proxies, or logging servers are used.

3. Local Browser Storage

The Extension uses chrome.storage.local strictly on your device to store user preferences (e.g. strictness level, blur toggle, preferred AI engine) and cached classifications. This data remains on your device and is never shared.

4. No Tracking & No Third-Party Sales

We do NOT track your browsing across the web, use analytics beacons, sell data to third parties or brokers, or serve advertisements.
